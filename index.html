Iron Rainbow

We send powder coating work to local shops.

Customers design their parts and pay online. The job goes to a coating partner near them.

How it works

  1. A customer configures their parts and colour in our 3D configurator.
  2. They pay up front — no quoting back-and-forth, no chasing invoices.
  3. The order lands with a local coating partner through our operations software.
  4. The shop coats the parts and keeps the large majority of the job value.

What a partner shop gets

Work with no customer acquisition cost, payment handled before the job starts, and scheduling and job tracking built in. You keep 80% of every job — we take a 10% platform fee for generating the demand and running the technology, and earmark 10% for the marketing that brings the work in.

Did we email you?

We maintain a directory of powder-coating and metal-finishing businesses, and we contact shops that are publicly listed in it. If you'd rather not hear from us, reply STOP to any message and you'll be removed immediately.
